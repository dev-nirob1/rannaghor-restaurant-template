About Rannaghor

A kitchen that smells like home

Rannaghor started with one family recipe book and a simple idea: serve food the way it is cooked at home, slowly and with the real spices.

Every morning our cooks grind fresh masala, marinate by hand and let the pot do its work. No shortcuts, no rush, just taste you remember.

  • Fresh every day

    Vegetables, fish and meat from local markets each morning.

  • Hand-ground spices

    Our masala is ground in-house, never from a packet.

  • Family recipes

    Passed down through three generations of home cooks.

  • Slow cooked

    Dum biryani and bhuna cooked for hours, not minutes.

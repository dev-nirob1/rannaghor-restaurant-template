Good to know

Questions we hear often

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but Friday evenings fill quickly. A booking a day ahead guarantees your table.

Can you host a large family gathering?

Yes, our private corner seats up to 20. For groups above 12, please call us so we can plan the menu together.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Plenty. Bhuna khichuri, dal bhuna, beguni and several bhortas are always on the menu, marked with a leaf.

Do you offer takeaway or delivery?

Takeaway is available all day. Call us on the number above and we will have it packed and hot when you arrive.

About us

Rannaghor means kitchen in Bangla. Everything we cook starts with that one word and the people who gather around it.

2012 Since
Our story

It started with a mother’s recipe notebook

In 2012 our founder opened a tiny eight-table room in Dhanmondi, with her mother’s stained notebook and a stubborn belief that Dhaka deserved real home cooking.

Today the room is bigger, but the rules are the same. Spices are ground each morning, gravies are cooked slowly and every dish leaves the kitchen only when someone from the family would happily eat it themselves.

  1. 2012

    The menu

What we stand for

Four promises at every table

  • Honest spice

    Whole spices, ground fresh. No shortcuts, no packets, no pre-made pastes.

  • Local first

    Rice, fish and vegetables come from farmers and fishers we know by name.

  • Slow fire

    Gravies simmered for hours and biryani sealed on dum, never hurried.

  • Family service

    You are a guest the moment you walk in, and family by the time you leave.

2012 Since

Our story

It started with a mother's recipe notebook.

In 2012 our founder opened a tiny eight-table room in Dhanmondi, armed with nothing but her mother's stained notebook and a stubborn belief that Dhaka deserved real home cooking.

Today the room is bigger, but the rules are the same. Spices are ground each morning, gravies are cooked slowly and every dish leaves the kitchen only when someone from the family would happily eat it themselves.

Our journey

Twelve years, one kitchen

  1. 2012

  2. 2016

    Kacchi Fridays

    Our Friday kacchi became the dish people queued for at the door.

  3. 2020

    A bigger table

    We moved into our current home with a proper open kitchen and a garden corner.

  4. 2024

    60 home recipes

    Every region of Bangladesh is now on our menu, cooked by cooks from those regions.
